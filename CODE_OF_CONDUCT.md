# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a friendly place to learn, build and disagree well.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Be welcoming to newcomers and patient with questions.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Explain the reasons behind a request.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the open, so others can learn from them.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, and personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space of the project, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/yzPeedro). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently block people who break this code.
